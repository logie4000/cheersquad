Cheer Squad

Plan where to stand so you see your runner as many times as possible.

Cheer Squad is a free marathon spectator planner. Pick a preloaded race or upload the course as a GPX file, enter your runner's goal finish time and pacing, and say when you can set off and how you'll get around: walking, bike, transit or car. It works out where your runner will be and when, then plans a route of cheering spots you can actually reach in time, with travel directions between them, a map, and a printable plan.
