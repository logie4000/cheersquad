How it works

Pick a race or upload the course as a GPX file, then enter your runner's goal finish time and when you can set off. Cheer Squad works out when your runner reaches each part of the course and plans the cheering spots you can get to in time on foot, by bike, by transit or by car, leaving each spot as soon as they pass.
